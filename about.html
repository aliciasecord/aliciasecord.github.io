The Archive of My Becoming

A living record of work, magic, and transformation.

Welcome to the crossroads, where spells, systems, and small obsessions conjure a life well lived. This page is a navel-gazey look at the whys and wherefores of this endeavor.

Hi. I'm Alicia. Well met.

I live a life that overflows: joy louder than sorrow, love spilling everywhere, a house full of kids and creatures and half-finished projects. Chaos is fine, but make it cozy. My kitchen feeds people. My garden grows pumpkins when it feels like it.

You've wandered into my corner of the internet. Here, I'm all my selves at once: the front-end technologist building systems for the industrial automation world, the Leatherfemme teaching alternative lifestyle classes, the fantasy writer, the dungeon master, the mother, the partner, the artist, the designer. Multitudes don't scare me. They just make the archive bigger.
